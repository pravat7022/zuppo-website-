ABOUT ZUPPO

Discover more in one place.

ZUPPO is an independent platform directory designed to help people discover online services across shopping, fashion, grocery, food, transport, travel, education and more.

Our Purpose

Our goal is to make discovering online platforms simpler and more convenient by bringing useful platform listings together in one place.

How ZUPPO Works

Choose a category, search for a platform and select a listing to visit the provider's official website.

Purchases, payments, bookings, deliveries and other services are handled by the respective providers, not by ZUPPO.
