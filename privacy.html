Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

Welcome to ZUPPO. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how ZUPPO handles information when you visit or use our website.

1. About ZUPPO

ZUPPO is an independent online platform directory. We help visitors discover shopping, fashion, food, travel and other online platforms and visit their official websites.

ZUPPO does not directly process purchases, payments, bookings or deliveries made on third-party platforms.

2. Information We May Process

Depending on the features you use, the following information may be processed:

  • Email address used for account registration or sign-in.
  • Authentication information needed to secure your account.
  • Technical information such as browser type, device information and IP address, where processed by our service providers.
  • Information related to website usage and advertising, where applicable.

We aim to collect or process only information needed to operate and improve the website.

3. Firebase Authentication

ZUPPO uses Firebase Authentication provided by Google to support email and password sign-in and Google Sign-In.

When you create an account or sign in, Firebase may process your email address, account identifier and authentication-related information to provide account access and security.

Firebase services are subject to Google's privacy practices and terms.

Learn more: Firebase Privacy and Security .

4. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Cookies are small files stored in your browser. They may be used to support website functionality, remember preferences, measure activity or support advertising services.

You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings. Some website features may not work properly if certain cookies are disabled.

5. Google AdSense and Advertising

ZUPPO uses or may use Google AdSense to display advertisements on the website, subject to Google's approval and advertising requirements.

Google and other third-party vendors may use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses or similar technologies to serve advertisements, measure ad performance and help prevent fraud.

Third-party vendors, including Google, may use information about your visits to this website and other websites to provide advertisements that may be relevant to your interests.

You can manage Google's personalized advertising preferences here: Google Ads Settings .

You can also learn how Google uses information from websites and apps that use its services: How Google uses information .

You may find additional information about interest-based advertising choices at: YourAdChoices .

6. Third-Party Websites

ZUPPO contains links to external websites and services operated by other companies.

When you visit an external website, that website's own privacy policy, terms and security practices apply. ZUPPO does not control how those external websites collect or use your information.

7. How Information May Be Used

Information may be processed for purposes such as:

  • Providing website and account functionality.
  • Maintaining account security.
  • Operating and improving the website.
  • Displaying and measuring advertisements.
  • Responding to support requests.
  • Meeting applicable legal requirements.

8. Information Sharing

Information may be processed by service providers that help operate ZUPPO, including Firebase and advertising providers such as Google.

These providers process information according to their applicable terms, privacy policies and legal requirements.

9. Data Security and Retention

We take reasonable steps to protect information processed through ZUPPO. However, no online service can guarantee complete security.

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, subject to applicable legal requirements and service-provider practices.

10. Your Privacy Choices

You may contact us with questions about your information or requests concerning your account. You can also manage cookies through your browser and advertising preferences through the controls provided by Google.

11. Children's Privacy

ZUPPO is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable privacy laws.
